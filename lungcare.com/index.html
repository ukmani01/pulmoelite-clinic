Identify early signs

Do you experience any of these?

Loud snoring

Habitual snoring, especially with pauses in breathing, is a key sleep apnoea marker.

Daytime fatigue

Excessive sleepiness, poor concentration, morning headache — signs of disrupted sleep.

High BP / Uncontrolled sugar

Sleep apnoea worsens blood pressure and insulin resistance significantly.

Breathlessness / Chronic cough

Persistent respiratory symptoms need specialist pulmonary evaluation.
